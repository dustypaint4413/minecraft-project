Interesting Facts

World Generation

Minecraft worlds are procedurally generated, meaning no two worlds are exactly alike. Each world can contain over 100 billion blocks!

Redstone

Redstone in Minecraft works like electricity, allowing players to create complex circuits and even basic computers within the game!

Explosive Accident

The creeper was a coding accident. Notch was writing the pig in August 2009 and swapped its height and length by mistake, producing a tall, four-legged creature instead. Rather than delete it, he coloured it green and gave it a temper.

Customization

Resource packs let players replace the game's textures, sounds, fonts and models, and behaviour packs can rewrite how mobs and items work. Thousands are freely available, so no two installations need look alike.

Endermen Speak

The eerie, garbled noises made by Endermen are actually English phrases played backward. If you reverse their audio files and shift the pitch, you can clearly hear them saying common phrases like "Hello" and "What's up?"

Scared Evokers

If an Evoker comes across a blue sheep in the wild, it will pause its attacks, wave its arms, and cast a secret spell accompanied by a "Wololo" sound effect. This spell magically dyes the sheep's wool red—a direct Easter egg paying homage to the classic real-time strategy game Age of Empires.
