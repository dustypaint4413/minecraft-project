Crafting Basics

Crafting Basics

Easy

Crafting is the foundation of progression in Minecraft. It is the process of combining raw materials to create useful items, tools, weapons, armor, and building blocks. Understanding how to navigate the crafting system is essential for surviving and advancing through the game.

The Crafting Grid
Minecraft features two distinct crafting interfaces:
The 2x2 Inventory Grid: Accessible at any time inside your inventory screen. It is used to craft basic, essential starter items like planks, sticks, torches, and crafting tables.
The 3x3 Crafting Table: Created by filling the 2x2 grid with planks. Placing this block down unlocks the full 3x3 layout required for almost every complex tool, weapon, and armor piece in the game.

How to Craft
Open the interface: Press your inventory key (E by default) to use the 2x2 grid, or interact with a placed Crafting Table to open the 3x3 grid.
Arrange the materials: Place the required ingredients into the grid slots. Many items require a specific physical pattern, while others can be placed anywhere.
Use the Recipe Book: Click the green book icon next to the grid to view all unlocked recipes and automatically place ingredients.
Collect the item: Click the finished item in the output box to pick it up, then place it into your inventory or hotbar.

Essential Recipes
Crafting Table: Place 4 wood planks in a 2x2 pattern.
Sticks: Place 2 wood planks vertically in a column (yields 4 sticks).
Chest: Place 8 wood planks around the entire outside edge of a 3x3 grid, leaving the center slot empty.
Torches: Place 1 coal or charcoal directly above 1 stick in a vertical column.
Furnace: Place 8 cobblestone or deepslate blocks around the edge of a 3x3 grid, leaving the center empty.

Tool Tiers and Materials
Wood: The introductory tier. Easy to gather but has very low durability and slow mining speeds.
Stone: Crafted using cobblestone. Much more durable than wood and required to mine iron ore.
Iron: Smelted from raw iron. Strong, fast, and able to mine high-tier blocks like diamond and gold.
Diamond: Extremely durable and fast. Found deep underground, it represents the pinnacle of overworld gear.
Netherite: The strongest material in the game. It is fireproof and created by upgrading diamond gear with a netherite ingot using a Smithing Table.

Important Tips
Craft smart: Use your Recipe Book to instantly craft maximum quantities of an item by holding Shift while clicking the output box.
Pick the right tool: Match your tool to the block type (pickaxes for stone/ores, axes for wood, shovels for dirt/sand, and hoes for crops) to avoid draining tool durability unnecessarily.
Keep a spare table: Always carry a crafting table in your inventory while exploring so you can craft emergency tools and blocks on the go.
