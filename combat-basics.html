Combat Basics

Combat Basics

Easy

Combat in Minecraft rewards timing far more than fast clicking. This guide covers the mechanics that decide how much damage you actually deal, the gear that keeps you alive, and how to handle the mobs you meet in your first week.

The Attack Cooldown
This is the single most important thing to learn. Since version 1.9 every weapon has a cooldown, shown as a bar on the crosshair. Attacking before it refills deals a fraction of the weapon's damage, so spam-clicking is actively worse than waiting. Let the bar fill, then strike. At roughly 85 percent charge you also unlock critical hits, sweep attacks and sprint-knockback attacks.

Critical Hits
Attacking while you are falling deals a critical hit, worth 150 percent of the weapon's damage. In practice that means jumping and striking on the way down. Combined with a full cooldown it is the highest damage a basic attack can do.

Swords and Axes
Swords recover faster and have a sweep attack that hits several mobs at once. Axes hit noticeably harder per swing but take longer to recharge, and they disable a shield for 5 seconds when they connect. Either works; the axe suits patient, single-target fights and the sword suits crowds.

Shields
Hold the use button to raise a shield. It blocks melee damage and most projectiles arriving from the front, which makes it the cheapest answer to skeletons. Two limits are worth knowing: it protects only the direction you are facing, and an axe hit disables it for 5 seconds.

Armour
Leather, then chainmail, then iron, then diamond, then netherite. Each piece contributes armour points, so a complete set of iron protects you better than one lone diamond piece. Repair armour on an anvil before it breaks rather than after.

Health and Food
You only regenerate health while your hunger bar is at 18 of 20 or higher, which is nine of the ten drumsticks. Fighting on a low hunger bar means fighting without healing, so carry food and eat before a fight rather than during one.

The Mobs You Will Meet First
Zombies: slow and predictable, and they burn in daylight. Back away while attacking so they cannot crowd you.
Skeletons: dangerous at range. Close the distance quickly or raise a shield and walk in.
Spiders: they jump and climb walls. They are neutral in daylight and hostile in the dark.
Creepers: silent until the hiss, then roughly one and a half seconds before the explosion. Hit once and step back immediately, or block with a shield. Never fight one in a corridor.

Practical Tips
Wait for the cooldown bar. It is worth more than any enchantment you have this early.
Fight in open ground where you can retreat, not in tunnels.
Keep a shield in your off-hand from the moment you can make one.
Sleep through the night when you can; the mobs you avoid cost nothing to beat.
