Mobs

Mobs

Guide

Every living thing in Minecraft is a mob, and the game sorts them by how they treat you. Passive mobs never fight back, neutral mobs retaliate only when provoked, hostile mobs attack on sight, and the two bosses are events in their own right. Knowing which group a creature belongs to tells you instantly whether to approach it, ignore it, or leave.

About the spawning percentages. Where the game gives a mob a spawn weight, the figure is that mob’s share of the spawn attempts on its list — sheep carry a weight of 12 against a cow’s 8 in the same grassy biomes, which is why sheep show 26% and cows 17%. The four common hostile mobs sit near 24% each because their weights are almost identical. Mobs that do not spawn by weight — those generated with a structure, produced by a spawner, or summoned by the player — have no such share, so their figure is an estimate of how rarely one appears at all. Minecraft publishes no single official rarity statistic, since spawning also depends on biome, light level, height and the mob cap.

Passive Mobs

These never attack you, though a pufferfish poisons anything that touches it and a snow golem will pelt hostile mobs on your behalf. Most can be bred with the right food, which is the difference between hunting animals forever and never worrying about food again.

  • Allay — Give one an item and it hunts down every matching drop within range, then brings the lot back to you. Play a note block nearby and it will deliver to that block instead, which makes it the simplest item sorter in the game. Dancing near a jukebox, it can be duplicated with an amethyst shard. Spawning percentage: 1%  ·  Found: Caged at pillager outposts and in woodland mansions  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Armadillo — Rolls into a ball when a player sprints close or a hostile mob approaches, and stays curled until the threat passes. Brushing one yields scutes without harming it, which is the only way to get them. Spawning percentage: 5%  ·  Found: Savanna biomes  ·  Drops: Armadillo scutes when brushed, used to craft wolf armour
  • Axolotl — Attacks drowned, guardians, squid and fish but never the player. Near death it plays dead, regaining health and becoming briefly untargetable, and any player fighting alongside one receives Regeneration. Spawning percentage: 3%  ·  Found: Water in lush caves  ·  Drops: Nothing; it can be carried in a bucket
  • Bat — Flies in erratic loops, sleeps hanging upside down, and does nothing else whatsoever. Its squeaks are often the first sign that a cave system is nearby. Spawning percentage: 15%  ·  Found: Dark areas underground  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Camel — Seats two players and stands tall enough that zombies and skeletons cannot reach the riders. It can dash forward over gaps and sits down when left idle. Spawning percentage: 2%  ·  Found: Desert villages  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Cat — Tamed with raw cod or salmon. A tamed cat sleeps on your bed, occasionally leaves a gift beside you in the morning, and keeps creepers and phantoms at a distance. Spawning percentage: 10%  ·  Found: Villages, with black cats in swamp huts  ·  Drops: Nothing; gifts include string, feathers and rabbit's feet
  • Chicken — Lays an egg every few minutes on its own, and flaps hard enough to negate all fall damage. Follows seeds of any kind, which makes it trivial to herd. Spawning percentage: 22%  ·  Found: Most grassy biomes  ·  Drops: Raw chicken and feathers
  • Cod and Salmon — Ordinary fish that suffocate if left on land. Both are far better cooked, and salmon restores noticeably more hunger than cod. Spawning percentage: 20%  ·  Found: Oceans and rivers, salmon preferring cold water  ·  Drops: Raw cod or salmon, occasionally bone meal
  • Tropical Fish — Generated in an enormous range of patterns and colours. Feeding them to axolotls is the only way to breed axolotls. Spawning percentage: 6%  ·  Found: Warm oceans and lush caves  ·  Drops: Tropical fish
  • Pufferfish — Inflates when anything comes near and poisons whatever touches it, including you. Its one real purpose is brewing Water Breathing. Spawning percentage: 3%  ·  Found: Warm oceans  ·  Drops: Pufferfish
  • Cow — Follows wheat, breeds easily, and supplies the leather that books, item frames and early armour all depend on. Milk it with an empty bucket to clear any status effect instantly. Spawning percentage: 17%  ·  Found: Grassy biomes  ·  Drops: Raw beef and leather
  • Mooshroom — A cow wearing mushrooms. Shear it for mushrooms, which permanently turns it into an ordinary cow, or milk it with a wooden bowl for stew. Lightning switches it between the red and brown variants. Spawning percentage: 0.5%  ·  Found: Mushroom fields only  ·  Drops: Raw beef and leather
  • Pig — Ride one with a saddle and steer it using a carrot on a stick. A pig struck by lightning becomes a zombified piglin. Spawning percentage: 22%  ·  Found: Grassy biomes  ·  Drops: Raw porkchop
  • Sheep — Regrows its wool by eating grass, so a single sheep is an unlimited wool supply. Dye the sheep rather than the wool and every future shearing comes out coloured. Spawning percentage: 26%  ·  Found: Most grassy biomes  ·  Drops: Wool and raw mutton
  • Rabbit — Small, quick and awkward to catch, with fur that matches the biome it spawned in. It eats carrot crops if you leave a farm unfenced. Spawning percentage: 8%  ·  Found: Deserts, snowy biomes, meadows and forests  ·  Drops: Raw rabbit, rabbit hide, rarely a rabbit's foot
  • Horse — Tame by mounting repeatedly until hearts appear, then fit a saddle. Speed, jump height and health vary considerably between individual horses, so it is worth testing several before committing. Spawning percentage: 11%  ·  Found: Plains and savannas  ·  Drops: Leather
  • Donkey and Mule — Slower than a horse, but each can wear a chest and carry cargo. A mule is bred from a horse and a donkey and cannot itself breed. Spawning percentage: 2%  ·  Found: Plains and savannas; mules only from breeding  ·  Drops: Leather
  • Skeleton Horse — Appears through a skeleton trap: lightning during a thunderstorm produces four of them ridden by skeletons. Once tamed it never drowns, which makes it useful underwater. Spawning percentage: 0.5%  ·  Found: Thunderstorms, rarely  ·  Drops: Bones
  • Ocelot — No longer tameable, but feeding it raw fish earns its trust so it stops fleeing from you. Its presence still keeps creepers away. Spawning percentage: 2%  ·  Found: Jungles  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Parrot — Tamed with seeds, rides on your shoulder, and imitates the sound of any hostile mob nearby, which makes it a crude early warning system. Never feed one a cookie; it is instantly fatal. Spawning percentage: 5%  ·  Found: Jungles  ·  Drops: Feathers
  • Frog — Three variants exist, temperate, warm and cold, decided by the biome the tadpole matures in rather than where the frog ends up. Each eats small slimes, which drop slimeballs, and small magma cubes, which produce a froglight in a colour determined by the frog variant. Spawning percentage: 5%  ·  Found: Swamps and mangrove swamps  ·  Drops: Nothing itself; froglights come from the magma cubes it eats
  • Tadpole — The juvenile frog, which must remain in water. Scoop one into a bucket and raise it in whichever biome produces the frog colour you want. Spawning percentage: 4%  ·  Found: Mangrove swamps, or hatched from frogspawn  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Squid — Swims in loose groups and releases a cloud of ink when hurt. Spawning percentage: 12%  ·  Found: Oceans and rivers  ·  Drops: Ink sacs, used for black dye and for books
  • Glow Squid — The glowing relative of the squid, and the only source of glow ink. Spawning percentage: 6%  ·  Found: Underground water, generally below Y:30  ·  Drops: Glow ink sacs, which make sign text glow in the dark
  • Turtle — Always returns to the exact beach where it hatched in order to lay eggs, so move it as an egg if you want a farm elsewhere. Eggs are fragile and most mobs trample them. Spawning percentage: 4%  ·  Found: Beaches  ·  Drops: Seagrass; growing babies drop scutes, and five scutes craft a turtle shell helmet
  • Sniffer — Hatched from a sniffer egg recovered by brushing suspicious sand in warm ocean ruins. It wanders slowly, sniffing the ground, and digs up ancient seeds that exist nowhere else in the game. Spawning percentage: 0.5%  ·  Found: Only by hatching an egg  ·  Drops: Nothing; it digs up torchflower seeds and pitcher pods
  • Strider — Walks calmly across lava and shivers and slows on solid ground. With a saddle and a warped fungus on a stick it becomes lava transport. Spawning percentage: 12%  ·  Found: Lava seas in the Nether  ·  Drops: String
  • Snow Golem — Built by the player from two snow blocks topped with a carved pumpkin. It throws snowballs that knock mobs back without hurting most of them, and leaves a trail of snow in cold biomes. Spawning percentage: 0.2%  ·  Found: Built only  ·  Drops: Snowballs
  • Villager — The entire trading economy of the game. Professions, levels, restocking and prices are covered in full on the Villagers and Trading page. Spawning percentage: 20%  ·  Found: Villages  ·  Drops: Nothing; the value is in its trades
  • Wandering Trader — Turns up near you for a limited time with two leashed trader llamas and an unpredictable selection of goods, then vanishes. It drinks invisibility at night. Spawning percentage: 8%  ·  Found: Anywhere, temporarily  ·  Drops: Milk bucket, and any leads it was using

Neutral Mobs

These attack only if provoked, and each has its own trigger. Learn the triggers and you can walk past every one of them safely.

  • Wolf — Tame with bones. A tamed wolf follows you, sits on command, and attacks whatever you attack. Strike a wild one and the whole pack retaliates. Wolf armour, crafted from armadillo scutes, keeps a tamed wolf alive. Spawning percentage: 7%  ·  Found: Forests, taigas and groves  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Enderman — Teleports the instant it is hit, takes damage from water and rain, and picks up certain blocks. It turns hostile only if you look directly at its head or attack it, and wearing a carved pumpkin prevents that entirely. Spawning percentage: 12%  ·  Found: Anywhere dark, and in enormous numbers in the End  ·  Drops: Ender pearls
  • Bee — Pollinates crops, which speeds their growth, then returns to its hive. Harvest honey or honeycomb with a campfire burning beneath the hive and the bees stay calm; without it they swarm, and a bee that stings dies shortly after. Spawning percentage: 6%  ·  Found: Flower forests, plains and meadows  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Dolphin — Follows boats and plays with floating items. Feed one raw cod and it leads you to the nearest shipwreck or ocean ruin, and swimming beside one grants a large speed boost. Spawning percentage: 5%  ·  Found: Oceans  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Goat — Rams anything it takes a dislike to, launching it a considerable distance, which is lethal near a cliff. If a ram connects with a solid block it may drop a horn. Screaming goats are a rarer, more aggressive variant. Spawning percentage: 5%  ·  Found: Mountain slopes, meadows and peaks  ·  Drops: Nothing; goat horns come from ramming
  • Llama and Trader Llama — Spits at whatever attacks it, and can be leashed into a caravan that follows a single leader. It takes a carpet as decoration and a chest for cargo. Spawning percentage: 4%  ·  Found: Savannas and mountains; trader llamas accompany wandering traders  ·  Drops: Leather
  • Panda — Generated with a personality, including lazy, playful, worried, weak and aggressive, and only the aggressive kind fights back. It eats bamboo and cake. Spawning percentage: 3%  ·  Found: Bamboo jungles  ·  Drops: Bamboo; sneezing babies drop slimeballs
  • Polar Bear — Ignores you completely until you approach a cub, at which point the adult charges immediately. Spawning percentage: 3%  ·  Found: Snowy biomes and frozen oceans  ·  Drops: Raw cod and raw salmon
  • Iron Golem — Spawns on its own in villages with enough villagers and beds, or is built from four iron blocks topped with a carved pumpkin. It attacks anything that harms a villager and hands poppies to village children. Spawning percentage: 15%  ·  Found: Villages, or built by the player  ·  Drops: Iron ingots and poppies
  • Piglin — Hostile on sight unless you wear at least one piece of gold armour. Drop a gold ingot near one and it inspects the ingot, then barters a random item back, which can include ender pearls, obsidian and fire resistance potions. Spawning percentage: 14%  ·  Found: Nether wastes, crimson forests and bastion remnants  ·  Drops: Gold and whatever it happened to be holding
  • Zombified Piglin — Completely neutral until one is attacked, at which point every zombified piglin within earshot swarms you at once. This is the most common cause of an unplanned Nether death. Spawning percentage: 25%  ·  Found: Throughout the Nether, and from a pig struck by lightning  ·  Drops: Rotten flesh and gold nuggets

Hostile Mobs

These attack on sight. Since the 1.18 update, hostile mobs in the Overworld can only spawn where the block light level is exactly 0, so lighting your base properly prevents nearly all of them.

  • Zombie — Burns in sunlight, drowns and converts into a drowned in water, and on Hard difficulty breaks wooden doors. One zombie that spots you calls others nearby, so a single sighting can become a crowd. Spawning percentage: 23%  ·  Found: Anywhere dark  ·  Drops: Rotten flesh, occasionally iron ingots, carrots or potatoes
  • Zombie Villager — A villager that was converted instead of killed, which happens most often on Hard. Cure it with a splash potion of Weakness followed by a golden apple, and you gain a villager whose trades are permanently discounted. Spawning percentage: 2%  ·  Found: Wherever zombies reach a village  ·  Drops: Rotten flesh
  • Husk — A desert zombie that does not burn in daylight, so it stays a threat around the clock, and its hits inflict Hunger. Spawning percentage: 6%  ·  Found: Deserts  ·  Drops: Rotten flesh
  • Drowned — Zombies converted underwater. Roughly one in sixteen carries a trident and throws it, which makes them the most dangerous ranged mob in the water. Spawning percentage: 14%  ·  Found: Oceans, rivers and underwater ruins  ·  Drops: Rotten flesh, occasionally a trident, nautilus shell or copper ingot
  • Skeleton — Fires arrows from a distance, strafes as it shoots, and burns in daylight unless it finds shade. The most lethal early mob precisely because it never has to come close. Spawning percentage: 24%  ·  Found: Anywhere dark  ·  Drops: Bones and arrows, occasionally its bow
  • Stray — A frozen skeleton whose arrows inflict Slowness, which makes retreating from one considerably harder. Spawning percentage: 5%  ·  Found: Snowy plains and icy biomes  ·  Drops: Bones, arrows and tipped arrows of Slowness
  • Bogged — A mossy skeleton that fires two arrows at a time, both inflicting Poison. Spawning percentage: 4%  ·  Found: Swamps, mangrove swamps and trial chambers  ·  Drops: Bones and arrows
  • Creeper — Approaches in near silence, hisses, then explodes about one and a half seconds later, destroying blocks and anything standing near them. Cats and ocelots frighten it away. One killed by a skeleton's arrow drops a music disc. Spawning percentage: 24%  ·  Found: Anywhere dark  ·  Drops: Gunpowder
  • Spider — Climbs sheer walls, leaps at you, and fits through gaps a single block high. Hostile in darkness and neutral in light, though one that turned hostile at night stays hostile after dawn. Spawning percentage: 22%  ·  Found: Anywhere dark  ·  Drops: String and spider eyes
  • Cave Spider — Smaller and faster than a spider, inflicts Poison, and slips through half-block gaps. It only ever comes from a spawner. Spawning percentage: 4%  ·  Found: Abandoned mineshafts  ·  Drops: String and spider eyes
  • Witch — Throws splash potions of Poison, Harming, Slowness and Weakness, and drinks Healing or Fire Resistance to undo your work. Fighting one slowly is a losing proposition. Spawning percentage: 3%  ·  Found: Swamp huts, raids, and rarely anywhere dark  ·  Drops: Glass bottles, glowstone dust, gunpowder, redstone, sugar, spider eyes and sticks
  • Slime — Appears in large, medium and small sizes, and each one splits into smaller copies when killed. Only the smallest actually die outright. Spawning percentage: 8%  ·  Found: Swamps at night, and in specific slime chunks below Y:40  ·  Drops: Slimeballs, from the smallest size only
  • Magma Cube — The Nether's answer to the slime, splitting in the same way and taking no damage from fire or lava. Spawning percentage: 7%  ·  Found: Nether wastes and basalt deltas  ·  Drops: Magma cream
  • Silverfish — Hides inside infested stone that looks completely ordinary. Breaking one block summons every silverfish nearby at once. Spawning percentage: 3%  ·  Found: Strongholds, mountains and igloo basements  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Endermite — Spawns rarely at the point where a thrown ender pearl lands. Endermen attack it on sight, which is what makes enderman farms possible. Spawning percentage: 0.5%  ·  Found: From thrown ender pearls  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Phantom — Spawns only once you have gone three in-game days without sleeping, dives at you from above, and burns at sunrise. Cats frighten them off, and sleeping prevents them entirely. Spawning percentage: 9%  ·  Found: Above ground at night  ·  Drops: Phantom membranes, used to brew Slow Falling
  • Guardian — Fires a slow charging laser that keeps hitting unless you break line of sight, and extends spikes that damage anyone striking it in melee. Spawning percentage: 4%  ·  Found: Ocean monuments  ·  Drops: Prismarine shards and crystals, raw cod
  • Elder Guardian — Three guard every monument. Each inflicts Mining Fatigue across a wide radius, which is precisely what stops you tunnelling straight in and taking the gold. Spawning percentage: 1%  ·  Found: Ocean monuments  ·  Drops: Prismarine shards and a wet sponge
  • Pillager — Carries a crossbow and travels in patrols. Killing a patrol captain gives you Bad Omen, which turns into a raid once you enter a village. Spawning percentage: 8%  ·  Found: Patrols, outposts and raids  ·  Drops: Arrows, occasionally its crossbow
  • Vindicator — An illager with an iron axe that hits harder than almost anything else at close range and closes distance quickly. One rare variant named Johnny attacks every mob it can reach. Spawning percentage: 3%  ·  Found: Woodland mansions and raids  ·  Drops: Emeralds, occasionally its axe
  • Evoker — Summons three vexes at a time, and conjures rows of biting fangs that erupt from the ground beneath you. Its drop is the reason people clear mansions at all. Spawning percentage: 1.5%  ·  Found: Woodland mansions and raids  ·  Drops: An emerald and a totem of undying, which revives you once at the moment you would have died
  • Vex — Small, fast, and able to fly straight through walls, so no amount of cover helps. It fades away after a time. Spawning percentage: 1.5%  ·  Found: Summoned by evokers  ·  Drops: Nothing
  • Ravager — Charges, deals heavy knockback, tramples crops, and stuns a raised shield outright. Illagers ride them during raids. Spawning percentage: 1.5%  ·  Found: Raids  ·  Drops: A saddle
  • Breeze — Fires wind charges that fling you and rearrange the fight rather than dealing much direct damage, and it bounces constantly, which makes hitting it difficult. Spawning percentage: 2%  ·  Found: Trial chambers  ·  Drops: Breeze rods, used to craft wind charges
  • Creaking — Bound to a creaking heart block hidden nearby. It takes no damage at all while that heart survives, and it freezes completely whenever a player looks at it, closing the distance only when unobserved. Spawning percentage: 1%  ·  Found: The pale garden  ·  Drops: Nothing; destroy its heart to stop it
  • Blaze — Hovers out of reach and fires volleys of three fireballs that set you alight, and it is immune to fire itself. Snowballs are unusually effective against it. Spawning percentage: 6%  ·  Found: Nether fortresses  ·  Drops: Blaze rods, the only source of blaze powder, so all brewing depends on them
  • Ghast — Drifts through open Nether space firing explosive fireballs, which can be hit back at it to kill it in one shot. Its cry carries for a very long distance. Spawning percentage: 10%  ·  Found: Nether wastes, soul sand valleys and basalt deltas  ·  Drops: Ghast tears and gunpowder
  • Wither Skeleton — Taller than an ordinary skeleton and armed with a stone sword. Its hits inflict Wither, which damages you straight through armour. Spawning percentage: 5%  ·  Found: Nether fortresses  ·  Drops: Bones and coal, and rarely a skull, three of which summon the Wither
  • Hoglin — Charges on sight and tosses you into the air. It is repelled by warped fungus and by nether portals, which is exactly how safe hoglin farms are built. Spawning percentage: 6%  ·  Found: Crimson forests  ·  Drops: Raw porkchop and leather
  • Zoglin — A hoglin brought into the Overworld, where it becomes permanently hostile to every mob including other zoglins, and can never breed or be calmed. Spawning percentage: 0.3%  ·  Found: Only from a transported hoglin  ·  Drops: Rotten flesh
  • Piglin Brute — Ignores gold armour entirely, cannot be distracted by bartering, and attacks with an axe. It guards the best loot in the Nether. Spawning percentage: 2%  ·  Found: Bastion remnants  ·  Drops: Its axe, occasionally
  • Shulker — Sits disguised as part of an end city wall until it opens and fires a homing projectile inflicting Levitation. The fall afterwards kills far more players than the shot itself. Spawning percentage: 2%  ·  Found: End cities  ·  Drops: Shulker shells, two of which turn a chest into a shulker box
  • Warden — Completely blind, hunting by vibration and smell alone. It emerges from the sculk after shriekers are triggered three times, hits harder than anything else in the game, and its ranged sonic attack passes straight through armour and shields. Wool muffles your footsteps and sneaking prevents vibrations entirely. Spawning percentage: 0.5%  ·  Found: The deep dark, beneath ancient cities  ·  Drops: A sculk catalyst

Bosses

Two mobs stand apart, with health bars across the top of the screen and rewards nothing else provides.

  • Ender Dragon — Circles the central island of the End and is healed continuously by end crystals mounted on the obsidian pillars, so those must be destroyed before any damage sticks. It perches on the central portal periodically, and that is the only reliable window for melee. Spawning percentage: 0.5%  ·  Found: The End  ·  Drops: An enormous amount of experience, the dragon egg on the first kill, and the gateway to the outer End islands
  • Wither — Never spawns naturally. You build it from four blocks of soul sand in a T shape topped with three wither skeleton skulls. It flies, fires explosive skulls, destroys terrain and inflicts Wither, and it attacks every mob nearby as well as you. Spawning percentage: 0.3%  ·  Found: Summoned by the player only  ·  Drops: The nether star, the one ingredient a beacon cannot be built without
