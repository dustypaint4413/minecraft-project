Dimensions

Dimensions

Guide

Minecraft has three worlds. The Overworld is where you start; the Nether and the End hold materials and progression you cannot get anywhere else. All three are covered below, with the mobs that live in each.

The Overworld
The dimension you spawn in, and the only one with a sky, a day and night cycle, and weather. It runs from bedrock at Y:-64 up to the build limit at Y:320, with sea level at Y:63 and deepslate replacing stone below Y:0.
Beds work here and nowhere else; in both other dimensions they explode when you try to sleep.
It is also the only dimension with animals you can breed, crops that grow, and villagers to trade with, which is why almost every base ends up here no matter how much time you spend elsewhere.
Its underground has biomes of its own: lush caves, dripstone caves, and the deep dark, which holds ancient cities and the warden.
Nearly every mob in the game lives here, so this is a summary rather than a list; the Mobs page covers all seventy-six in full.
Passive: Cows, pigs, sheep, chickens, rabbits, horses, villagers and the rest of the farm and forest animals, with fish, squid, turtles, dolphins and axolotls in the water.
Neutral: Wolves, endermen, bees, goats, llamas, pandas, polar bears and iron golems.
Hostile: Zombies, skeletons, creepers and spiders as the constant four, plus witches, slimes, drowned, husks, strays, bogged, phantoms, silverfish and cave spiders.
Illagers: Pillagers, vindicators, evokers and ravagers, which come from outposts, mansions and raids rather than ordinary spawning.
Found nowhere else: The warden, every illager, and every animal you can breed.

The Nether
You reach it through a portal built from a frame of obsidian, at minimum four blocks wide by five tall, with the corners optional, lit with flint and steel.
Distance runs at a ratio of one to eight: one block travelled in the Nether moves you eight in the Overworld, which makes it the fastest long-distance transport in the game.
Nothing behaves normally there. Water evaporates instantly, beds explode if you try to sleep, compasses and clocks spin uselessly, and there is no day or night.
Its biomes are the nether wastes, crimson and warped forests, soul sand valleys and basalt deltas, each with its own hazards and mobs.
You go for blaze rods from fortresses, which brewing depends on, for netherite and gold from bastions, and for quartz, which is everywhere.
No passive animals live here at all, so there is nothing to hunt for food except hoglins.
Zombified Piglin: Everywhere, and neutral until one of them is struck.
Piglin: Nether wastes, crimson forests and bastions. Hostile unless you wear at least one piece of gold.
Piglin Brute: Bastion remnants only, and gold does not protect you from it.
Hoglin: Crimson forests and bastions, and the only source of meat in the dimension.
Ghast: Nether wastes, soul sand valleys and basalt deltas.
Blaze: Nether fortresses, almost always from spawners.
Magma Cube: Basalt deltas above all, and nether wastes.
Wither Skeleton: Nether fortresses, and the only source of the skulls that summon the Wither.
Skeleton: Soul sand valleys, where ordinary skeletons replace most other spawns.
Strider: On the open lava seas, and rideable with a saddle and a warped fungus on a stick.
Enderman: Warped forests in particular, and scattered through the wastes and soul sand valleys.

The End
Reaching it takes twelve eyes of ender at most: you throw some to locate a stronghold, and use the rest to fill the portal frame.
The central island holds the Ender Dragon, healed by crystals mounted on obsidian pillars. Destroy the crystals first, or the fight cannot be won.
Beating the dragon opens a gateway to the outer islands, where end cities hold enchanted gear, shulkers, and on an end ship the elytra, the only means of flight in survival.
Beds explode here too. Endermen are everywhere, so a pumpkin worn on your head, which stops them reacting to your gaze, makes the whole dimension calmer.
Only four creatures exist here, and three of them are related.
Ender Dragon: One per world, circling the central island.
Enderman: Everywhere, in far greater numbers than the Overworld. A carved pumpkin makes the whole dimension safe to cross.
Shulker: Attached to the walls of end cities on the outer islands.
Endermite: Not a natural spawn; it appears occasionally where a thrown ender pearl lands.
