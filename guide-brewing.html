Brewing

Brewing

Guide

Brewing turns raw materials into potions, and potions are what make the hardest parts of the game survivable. Fire Resistance changes the Nether from lethal to routine, and Water Breathing does the same for ocean monuments.

Setting Up
Brewing stand: Crafted from one blaze rod and three cobblestone, cobbled deepslate or blackstone. The blaze rod means a trip to a nether fortress before you can brew anything at all.
Fuel: Blaze powder, crafted from blaze rods, goes in the left slot. One piece fuels twenty operations.
Bottles: Craft glass bottles from glass, fill them at any water source or cauldron, and place up to three in the bottom slots so you brew three at a time.

The Base Potion
Nearly every recipe starts from an Awkward Potion, which is a water bottle brewed with nether wart. Nether wart grows on soul sand in nether fortresses, and a small farm of it at home saves endless trips.
The Potion of Weakness is the sole exception: it is brewed straight into a water bottle with no nether wart at all.

Every Potion

Each of these starts from an awkward potion unless stated otherwise. Brewing three at once costs the same single ingredient as brewing one, so always fill all three slots.

  • Swiftness — Increases movement speed. Cheap enough to keep a stack of, and the usual choice for long overland journeys. Ingredient: Sugar
  • Strength — Adds flat melee damage to every hit. The difference between a hard fight and a short one. Ingredient: Blaze powder
  • Healing — Restores health instantly rather than over time, which is what you want mid-fight. It also damages the undead. Ingredient: Glistering melon
  • Regeneration — Heals steadily over its duration. Better than Healing when you are not being hit at that moment. Ingredient: Ghast tear
  • Fire Resistance — Complete immunity to fire and lava while it lasts. The single most useful potion in the game, and what makes the Nether routine. Ingredient: Magma cream
  • Night Vision — Lights the world as though it were day, without placing a single torch. Invaluable underwater and in caves. Ingredient: Golden carrot
  • Water Breathing — Removes the need to surface. Brew it before any ocean monument. Ingredient: Pufferfish
  • Leaping — Raises jump height and reduces fall damage slightly. Ingredient: Rabbit's foot
  • Slow Falling — Cancels fall damage entirely while it lasts. Carry one whenever you fly with an elytra. Ingredient: Phantom membrane
  • Poison — Damages over time but never kills outright. Mainly brewed as a step towards Harming. Ingredient: Spider eye
  • The Turtle Master — Heavy damage resistance at the cost of severe slowness. Situational, but it survives things nothing else does. Ingredient: Turtle shell
  • Weakness — Reduces melee damage. The only potion in the game brewed without nether wart, and half of what cures a zombie villager. Ingredient: Fermented spider eye, straight into a water bottle
  • Invisibility — Hides you from most mobs, though held items and worn armour remain visible. Ingredient: Night Vision plus fermented spider eye
  • Harming — Instant damage, and it heals the undead rather than hurting them. Ingredient: Healing or Poison plus fermented spider eye
  • Slowness — Slows the target. Most useful thrown as a splash potion. Ingredient: Swiftness or Leaping plus fermented spider eye

Modifiers
Redstone dust: Extends the duration.
Glowstone dust: Strengthens the effect, but shortens it. You cannot have both.
Gunpowder: Converts the potion into a splash potion you throw at others.
Dragon's breath: Converts a splash potion into a lingering one that leaves a cloud behind. Collected from the Ender Dragon's attacks with an empty bottle.
Fermented spider eye: Corrupts a potion into its opposite, as in the three entries at the end of the list above.

Worth Brewing First
Fire Resistance before any serious Nether trip, Water Breathing before an ocean monument, and Slow Falling before you take an elytra anywhere.
