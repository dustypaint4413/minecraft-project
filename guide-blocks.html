Blocks

Blocks

Guide

Blocks are the world. Everything you stand on, mine, build with or switch on is one, and the differences between them decide how the game plays.

Natural Blocks
Stone and deepslate make up the bulk of the underground, deepslate taking over below roughly Y:0 and being noticeably slower to mine. Dirt, grass, sand, gravel and clay cover the surface.
Sand, red sand, gravel and concrete powder are affected by gravity and fall when unsupported, which is worth remembering before you tunnel underneath them.

Ores
Coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis lazuli, diamond and emerald each generate at their own depths, and each has a deepslate variant that takes longer to break. Every ore needs a minimum pickaxe tier: iron for diamond and gold, stone for iron and copper.

Wood
Each tree species gives a complete matching set: logs, planks, stairs, slabs, fences, doors, trapdoors, signs and boats. Stripping a log with an axe gives a different texture again, which is the cheapest way to add variety to a build.

Functional Blocks
The crafting table, furnace, chest and bed are the four you need first. Beyond them sit the smoker and blast furnace, which are faster than a furnace for food and ore respectively, the anvil, brewing stand, enchanting table, cauldron, hopper, and the beacon, which grants area effects once you have a nether star.

Blast Resistance
Obsidian withstands creeper and TNT explosions and is the material for nether portals and enchanting tables. Bedrock cannot be broken at all in survival and forms the floor of the world and the ceiling of the Nether.

Waterlogging
Many blocks, including stairs, slabs, fences and lanterns, can hold water inside themselves rather than displacing it. This is what lets you light or decorate an underwater build without draining it first.

Block List

Every obtainable block, built up family by family rather than copied from a list, so the colour sets and wood sets are complete: all sixteen colours of wool, concrete, terracotta, glass, beds, banners, candles and shulker boxes, and the full set of planks, logs, stairs, slabs, fences, doors, signs and trapdoors for every wood. Technical blocks, category pages and items are left out. It runs to 1,002 entries, so use your browser's find function rather than reading it through.
Two entries, the end portal and end gateway, show an outlined tile rather than a picture. Those two have no block texture at all: the game draws them as an animated effect instead.
