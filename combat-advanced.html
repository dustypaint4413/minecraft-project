Advanced Combat

Advanced Combat

Hard

Advanced combat is about the fights that can end a world: bosses, raids, and the deep dark. This guide covers the gear ceiling, the mechanics that stop being invisible at this level, and how each of the game's set-piece encounters actually works.

The Gear Ceiling
Netherite is the end of the progression: higher toughness than diamond, some built-in knockback resistance, and it survives lava. Pair it with Protection IV on every piece, Sharpness V or Smite V on the weapon, and Unbreaking III with Mending so the set repairs itself from experience you were collecting anyway.

Damage Immunity
After taking a hit, an entity is invulnerable for roughly half a second. This is why click speed does nothing and why the cooldown bar matters: extra hits landed inside that window are simply discarded. It also means one heavy, fully charged strike beats three rushed ones.

Totem of Undying
Dropped by evokers, which appear in woodland mansions and in later raid waves. Held in the off-hand, a totem revives you once at low health with Regeneration and Absorption. Carry one into every fight in this guide.

The Wither
Summoned with soul sand or soul soil and three wither skeleton skulls, which drop rarely from wither skeletons in Nether fortresses. It explodes on spawning, so build the summoning point somewhere prepared and enclosed, never near your base. The Wither is undead, so Smite applies. Keep Regeneration running and stay out of the open.

The Ender Dragon
The end crystals on top of the obsidian pillars heal the dragon, so nothing else matters until they are gone. Shoot the exposed ones with a bow; the caged ones have to be reached and broken by hand. Once the crystals are down, the dragon can be damaged when it perches on the central portal.
Beds explode in the End, which makes them a well-known high-damage option against the perched dragon. Place, then trigger from cover, and never while standing next to one.

Raids
Killing a raid captain gives you Bad Omen. Entering a village with that effect starts a raid. Waves escalate through pillagers, vindicators, witches, evokers and ravagers. Fight from a defensible position with clear sightlines, keep the villagers behind you, and expect evokers to summon vexes that ignore walls.

The Warden
Found in the deep dark. It is blind and hunts by sound and by the vibrations sculk sensors pass to it. It has far more health than anything else in the game and can damage you through full netherite, so treat it as a hazard rather than an opponent.
Sneak everywhere, place wool to muffle vibrations, and throw snowballs or arrows away from yourself to pull its attention. The correct outcome of meeting a warden is leaving with the loot, not a kill.

Mobility as a Weapon
An elytra with fireworks turns positioning into something you control completely: disengage from a losing fight, reposition above a raid, or cross a boss arena instantly. Note that firework rockets with firework stars deal damage, so they double as a ranged option.

Practical Tips
Never enter a set-piece fight without a totem in the off-hand.
Bring more food than you think you need; healing is gated behind your hunger bar.
Fire Resistance trivialises large parts of the Nether and the blaze fights that gate brewing.
Practise the cooldown rhythm before it matters. Every mechanic above assumes you already have it.
