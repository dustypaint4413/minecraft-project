Biomes

Biomes

Guide

A biome is a region with its own terrain, weather, plants and mobs. Which biome you settle in decides what you can gather without travelling, so the choice matters more than it first appears.

Temperate Biomes
Plains: Flat, open and full of animals. Villages are common here, and building is easy.
Forest and birch forest: Dense wood, which is exactly what you need for the first few days.
Swamp: Slimes at night, clay, mangrove wood in the mangrove variant, and witches in their huts.
Cherry grove: Pink foliage and a wood set of its own.

Cold Biomes
Taiga: Spruce wood, wolves, foxes and sweet berries. Snowy variants add igloos.
Mountain slopes and peaks: Where emeralds and goats live. Snow falls instead of rain above a certain height.

Hot and Dry Biomes
Desert: No water and almost no wood, but temples, villages and unlimited sand for glass.
Savanna: Acacia wood and flat, easily built ground.
Badlands: The best place in the game for gold, which generates far more often and much higher up here than anywhere else.

Jungle
Difficult to move through, but the only place cocoa beans grow, and the home of jungle temples. Bamboo and pandas belong to the bamboo jungle variant, though bamboo also turns up in shipwreck and jungle temple chests.

Ocean and Underground
Oceans range from warm reefs to frozen. Their floors hold shipwrecks, buried treasure and ocean monuments.
Cave biomes generate below ground: lush caves with glow berries and axolotls, dripstone caves with pointed dripstone, and the deep dark, which contains ancient cities and the warden.

Choosing a Base Site
The most convenient place to settle is a border between biomes. A plains and forest edge next to a mountain gives you food, wood and stone within a short walk, and a river nearby means water without hauling it.
