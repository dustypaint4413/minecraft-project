Structures

Structures

Guide

Structures are the buildings and formations the world generator places for you to find. They are where the best loot lives, and most of the game's progression runs through them: no fortress means no brewing, no stronghold means no End, and no end ship means no elytra.

Overworld Structures

Everything the world generator places above and below the surface of the Overworld.

  • Village — Inhabited by villagers, with beds, crop farms, job site blocks and usually an iron golem on patrol. It is the safest place to spend an early night and the entry point to the whole trading economy. Found: Plains, deserts, savannas, taigas and snowy plains  ·  Loot: Chests vary by biome: bread, emeralds, iron tools, saddles and occasionally an enchanted book
  • Pillager Outpost — A tall watchtower surrounded by pillagers, sometimes with a caged allay. Killing the captain grants Bad Omen, which turns into a raid at the next village you enter. Found: Any biome where villages generate  ·  Loot: Crossbows, arrows, iron, and dark oak logs
  • Desert Temple — A sandstone pyramid whose real chamber is hidden beneath the floor. A stone pressure plate sits directly above nine blocks of TNT, so break in from the side rather than dropping down the middle. Found: Deserts  ·  Loot: Diamonds, gold, emeralds, enchanted books and horse armour
  • Jungle Temple — A mossy cobblestone temple defended by tripwires and arrow dispensers, with a three-lever puzzle guarding the lower chest. Cut the tripwire with shears rather than walking into it. Found: Jungles  ·  Loot: Diamonds, gold, emeralds, bamboo and horse armour
  • Mineshaft — Sprawling wooden tunnels held up by scaffolding, threaded with rails and chest minecarts, and frequently guarded by cave spider spawners hidden in cobwebs. Found: Underground everywhere; badlands variants generate much closer to the surface  ·  Loot: Rails, iron, gold, redstone, lapis and occasionally a golden apple
  • Stronghold — The only place the End portal generates. You find one by throwing eyes of ender and following the direction they fly, then digging down where they start to descend. Found: Underground, in rings around the world spawn  ·  Loot: Library books, enchanted books, iron, and the End portal itself
  • Ocean Monument — A prismarine fortress guarded by guardians and three elder guardians. Their Mining Fatigue is what stops you simply tunnelling in, so bring milk or clear the elders first. Found: Deep oceans  ·  Loot: Hidden gold blocks, wet sponges and large quantities of prismarine
  • Woodland Mansion — An enormous dark oak mansion packed with evokers and vindicators, and riddled with hidden rooms that hold the best chests. Usually thousands of blocks from spawn. Found: Dark forests, extremely rare  ·  Loot: Totems of undying from evokers, enchanted gear and diamonds
  • Ancient City — A sculk-covered city in the deep dark, holding the strongest loot in the Overworld. Trip a shrieker three times and the warden arrives, so move on wool and stay crouched. Found: The deep dark biome, deep underground  ·  Loot: Enchanted golden apples, diamonds, echo shards, and Swift Sneak, which exists nowhere else
  • Shipwreck — A wrecked vessel lying on the seabed or beached in the sand, with up to three chests spread across the hull. The map chest is the one worth finding. Found: Oceans and beaches  ·  Loot: Buried treasure maps, iron, emeralds and food; the map leads to a chest holding a heart of the sea

Nether Structures

Fewer in number, but each one gates a material you cannot progress without.

  • Nether Fortress — Dark brick corridors and open bridges spanning the lava seas. The only reliable source of blaze rods, which every potion in the game ultimately depends on. Found: Anywhere in the Nether  ·  Loot: Blaze rods from blazes, wither skeleton skulls, saddles, gold, and nether wart from its gardens
  • Bastion Remnant — A blackstone piglin stronghold, and considerably more dangerous than a fortress because of the brutes, which ignore gold armour entirely. Found: Anywhere in the Nether  ·  Loot: Netherite scrap, ancient debris, gold blocks, enchanted books and the Pigstep music disc
  • Ruined Portal — A broken obsidian frame with a chest beside it, generating in both dimensions. Often the cheapest way to get your first obsidian and a flint and steel. Found: The Overworld and the Nether, at any height  ·  Loot: Obsidian, flint and steel, gold, and occasionally an enchanted golden apple

End Structures

The reward for finishing the dragon fight.

  • End City — Purpur towers on the outer End islands, guarded by shulkers whose levitation shots are more dangerous than the fall. An end ship moored alongside carries the elytra. Found: The outer End islands, reached through the gateway after the dragon  ·  Loot: Enchanted diamond gear, shulker shells, and the elytra from an end ship
